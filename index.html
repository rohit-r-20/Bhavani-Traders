About Bhavani Traders

Hardware supplier in Chennai with a dedicated focus on heavy-duty clamps and dependable hardware supplies.

Bhavani Traders is a hardware supplier based in Chennai, specializing in heavy-duty clamps and dependable hardware supplies.

The business focuses on heavy-duty clamps designed for dependable, long-term use and resistance to early rusting. We also supply related hardware and structural fasteners to tradespeople, workshops, and builders.

We prioritize direct, straightforward service. To confirm product availability or discuss clamp requirements for your work, call our store directly or visit during listed business hours.
